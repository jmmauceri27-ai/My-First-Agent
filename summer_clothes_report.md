## Fit Notes for 6'3" / 195 lbs

- **Shirt size Large** runs true at Buck Mason, Vince, Sunspel, and Reiss. Norse Projects fits slightly slim — size up to XL if you have broad shoulders.
- **33/32 pants** are well-stocked online at Theory, Todd Snyder, Bonobos, and J.Crew. Bonobos lets you freely mix waist and inseam sizes, making it the safest bet for exact fit.
- The biggest challenge at 6'3" is **torso length in t-shirts** — Buck Mason's curved hem and Faherty's dedicated Tall sizing directly address this.
- Avoid relaxed-fit or "regular" cuts at all costs — at your proportions, slim and tailored fits read as intentional; relaxed fits read as ill-fitting.
- **Inseam for shorts:** A 7" inseam hits mid-thigh cleanly on long legs. A 5" inseam can look too short; a 9" can drag toward the knee.
