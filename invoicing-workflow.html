Invoicing Workflow

Client: C&W Services — Amazon  |  Vendor: DVM Fire

1
Receive Invoice from Accounts Receivable AR sends the original invoice with a reference number.
2
Create Receivable Invoice on UtilizeCore Open the relevant work order on UC and create the receivable invoice.
3
Fill Custom Invoice # with Reference # Enter the reference # from the AR invoice into the Custom Invoice # field on UC.
4
Break Out Line Items to Amazon Rate Card Match each line item to the corresponding rate from the Amazon contract rate card.
5
Attach PDF of Original AR Invoice Upload the original invoice PDF as an internal attachment on the work order.
⚠ Internal only — do NOT make available to client
6
Send Invoice to C&W Submit through both platforms:
Corrigo
UtilizeCore
7
Create Payable to DVM Fire Create a payable matching the same amount as the receivable. Fill the Custom Invoice # with the same reference # used in step 3.
8
Change Work Order Status to "Invoiced" Update the work order status on UtilizeCore to complete the process.
✓ Process complete
